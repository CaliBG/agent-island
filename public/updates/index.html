0.3.110 · Agent follow-ups and desktop widgets

November 27, 2026 · Apple silicon Mac and Windows x64

0.3.109 · Desktop widgets

November 27, 2026 · Apple silicon Mac and Windows x64

Already purchased? Download either installer from your original Creem order. On a supported Mac, the update prompt in AISLAND offers the latest release.

  • Running agents. A capsule beside the + button pairs the orbit animation with a live count. The number rolls up as agents start and down as they finish. Multiple sessions from the same agent count once.
  • Custom widget layouts. Use + on the dashboard or the expanded island to add, remove, resize and reorder widgets. Each surface remembers its own layout.
  • Session runways. Choose which conversations appear under each agent. Expand an agent to see its animated lanes, or place another widget between two agent groups.
  • Model spending. Compare estimated costs over the last 7 days, the last 30 days or your full recorded history. Bars rise in date order when you switch ranges.
  • Output speed. The tokens-per-second widget lets you choose how often to check for new samples.
  • Cache hits. See what share of measured input tokens came from cache when your usage records include cache counts.
  • System readings. Add CPU load, memory, battery or chip temperature alongside your agents. Larger battery widgets show the charge trend. Temperature readings depend on the sensors your computer exposes.
  • Preferences. Agent connections and other settings now sit under five tabs across the top of Preferences.
